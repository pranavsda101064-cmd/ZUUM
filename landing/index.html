Questions

Before you install.

The short answers about your first ride or parcel.

Still stuck? Reach the team

Where does Zuum work?

Zuum is built for Bengaluru. The app supports rides and goods across the city, with more cities planned.

What can I book?

Bikes, autos, hatchbacks, sedans and SUVs for rides. For goods: 2W parcel, 3W cargo, Tata Ace, pickup, van and tempo.

How is my fare decided?

Zuum shows an upfront fare based on distance, time and demand before you book. The breakdown stays visible on the receipt.

Can I send a parcel without travelling?

Yes. Add pickup and drop, receiver details and a goods vehicle. Delivery closes with a delivery OTP and photo proof.

What payment methods work?

Cash, UPI and the Zuum wallet are part of the app. Wallet top ups and trip payments stay in your account history.

Is there safety support?

Share your live trip, keep SOS one tap away, and reach support from the trip screen.

What about iOS?

Zuum is Android first. The iOS build is not available yet.

How do I install the app?

Tap Install for Android. If the public build has not landed yet, the button tells you instead of sending you to a dead file.
