Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Zuum is a mobility and logistics app for Bengaluru. This page explains what the customer and partner apps collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It covers both Zuum and Zuum Partner.

What we collect

  • Account details. Name, email address, phone number and sign in credentials.
  • Location. Pickup and drop points you enter, and live device location while a trip is active or a partner is online.
  • Trip records. Routes, fares, vehicle category, trip status, receipts, ratings and support messages.
  • Payments. Payment method, wallet ledger entries, payout details and transaction status. Full payment credentials stay with the payment provider.
  • Partner verification. Driving licence, vehicle registration, insurance and KYC documents submitted by partners and fleet owners.
  • Device and usage data. App version, device type and diagnostic events used to keep the service working.

How we use it

  • Match customers with nearby drivers and goods vehicles.
  • Calculate and show fares, routes and arrival times.
  • Run trip safety tools such as live sharing, SOS and support.
  • Process payments, partner payouts and commission records.
  • Verify partner documents and prevent misuse of the platform.
  • Improve routing, reliability and the app experience.

We do not sell personal data.

What is shared

A driver receives the pickup and drop details needed to complete your trip, along with a masked phone number. Map, routing, place search and payment providers process data on our behalf so the app can work. Partner KYC documents are available to authorised review staff. We may disclose data when the law requires it or to protect the safety of users.

How long we keep it

Account data stays while your account is active. Trip, payment and KYC records are kept for the period needed for accounting, dispute resolution and legal obligations. Support conversations are kept long enough to resolve the issue and improve the service.

Your choices

  • Turn location permission off in your device settings, though core trip features will not work without it.
  • Update your profile and saved places inside the app.
  • Ask for a copy, correction or deletion of your account data through the contact channel below.

Security

Access to personal data is limited to the people and systems that need it. Traffic between the apps and the Zuum API uses encrypted transport. No system is perfect, so report a suspected issue as soon as you find one.

Children

Zuum is not intended for people under 18.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the way we handle data changes. For a privacy request, open an issue at github.com/pranavsda101064-cmd/ZUUM/issues.
