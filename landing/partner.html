Questions

Before you sign in.

The short answers for new partners and fleet owners.

Still stuck? Reach the team

Who can drive with Zuum?

Anyone with a valid driving licence, vehicle documents and completed KYC can apply to drive in Bengaluru.

Which vehicles can I add?

Bike, auto, hatchback, sedan or SUV for rides. 2W parcel, 3W cargo, Tata Ace, pickup, van or tempo for goods.

When do I see my payout?

Before you accept. The offer card shows the fare, platform fee and net payout for that trip.

How do payouts work?

Completed trips collect in your earnings ledger. Payouts run on a weekly cycle, and the ledger keeps every trip visible.

Do I need to own a vehicle?

You can drive your own vehicle, or a fleet owner can assign one to you in the fleet console.

Can fleet owners use Zuum?

Yes. The fleet console covers the driver roster, vehicle assignment and earnings review.

What documents do I need?

Driving licence, vehicle registration, insurance and payout details. KYC status stays visible in the app.

How do I install the partner app?

Tap Install Zuum Partner. If the public build has not landed yet, the button tells you instead of sending you to a dead file.
